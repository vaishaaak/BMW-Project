🚗 BMW Sales Analysis & Excel Dashboard (2010–2024)

📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes BMW sales data from 2010 to 2024 using Microsoft Excel. The workbook contains 50,000 sales records covering BMW models, regions, vehicle specifications, pricing, mileage, sales volume, and sales classification.

The project demonstrates practical Excel data analysis, PivotTables, charts, dashboard design, and business insight generation.

🎯 Project Objectives

Analyze BMW sales performance across multiple years.

Compare sales volume across BMW models and regions.

Understand customer preferences by fuel type, transmission, and color.

Examine relationships between vehicle price, mileage, engine size, and sales.

Identify high- and low-sales classifications.

Build interactive dashboards and summary reports using Excel.

Present business insights in a clear and visual format.

🔎 Sample Dataset Insights

Based on the source data:

The dataset contains 50,000 records across 11 BMW models.

The data covers 15 years, from 2010 through 2024.

Total recorded sales volume is approximately 253.38 million.

The average listed vehicle price is approximately $75,035.

Approximately 30.5% of records are classified as High sales.

Asia has the highest total recorded sales volume among the six regions.

7 Series has the highest total recorded sales volume among the BMW models in this dataset.

Hybrid vehicles have the highest recorded sales volume among the four fuel types.

Manual and Automatic transmission sales volumes are relatively close in the dataset.

2022 records the highest annual sales volume among the years represented.

These insights describe the provided dataset and should not be interpreted as official BMW company sales figures.
